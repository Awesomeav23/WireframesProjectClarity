How rate-card values are calculated

All values shown below are mock numbers. In production, FLC comes from Finance's monthly calculation and Bill Rate comes from DynPro's standard rate card.

Internal Only

FLC Rate (Fully Loaded Cost)

FLC per hour = Annual Total Cost ÷ Annual Billable Hours

Example: Data Architect, Onshore (US)

Base salary$120,000
Benefits (health, retirement, PTO)$22,000
Payroll taxes (employer portion)$11,000
Overhead allocation$18,000
Annual Total Cost$171,000
÷ Annual Billable Hours1,900
FLC Rate$171,000 ÷ 1,900 = $90/hr

Example: Data Architect, Offshore (India)

Base salary (USD-equivalent)$40,000
Benefits + payroll taxes$8,000
Overhead allocation$42,000
Annual Total Cost$90,000
÷ Annual Billable Hours1,800
FLC Rate= $50/hr
Customer-facing

Bill Rate

Bill Rate is not calculated by the spreadsheet — it's a manually-entered number, the price the customer agreed to pay per hour. Sales sets it deal-by-deal based on the customer-negotiated rate.

In production, Sales would set a default per customer (often pulled from the MSA), but the user can always edit it. The spreadsheet never auto-updates Bill Rate when role or location changes.

Internal Only

Total Cost (per row in the spreadsheet)

Total Cost = Total Hours × FLC Rate
where Total Hours = Hrs/Week × Number of Weeks (from Start Date to End Date)

Once both Total Cost and Total Fees are known, Margin $ = Total Fees − Total Cost and Margin % = (Margin $ ÷ Total Fees) × 100. If overall Margin % drops below 40%, the SOW routes to SLT approval.

Cadence

When and how often invoices go out.

What these mean:
Milestone-based — no regular schedule. An invoice goes out only when a specific milestone is completed and signed off (e.g. "Phase 1 design accepted"). Common for Fixed Fee or Hybrid SOWs where payment is tied to deliverables.
On completion — one single invoice at the very end of the project, after everything is delivered and accepted. Used only for short engagements or special customer agreements.
